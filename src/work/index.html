Frequently asked questions

How long until the site is live?

After confirming the template, payment, and materials (logo, copy, photos), average delivery is 48 business hours for Starter, up to 5 business days for Business, and up to 10 business days for Corporate.

What do I need to send?

Logo, photos (if you have them), contact info, and a summary of what your business does. If anything is missing, we help you organize — without blocking the project.

Will the site be in my name?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, and so is hosting. You keep full control. We just set it up and publish.

How does payment work?

50% upfront to start the project, 50% on delivery before publishing. We accept Wise, Stripe, PayPal, and international bank transfer.

Can I request changes after delivery?

Yes. Revision rounds included in the package are covered. Larger changes after publishing are billed hourly — always agreed in advance. Clients on the maintenance plan get small monthly tweaks included.

Do I need the maintenance plan?

No. The site is truly yours — you can host it wherever you want. The plan covers managed hosting, WhatsApp support, and small ongoing tweaks for those who prefer not to worry about the technical side.

I don't have a logo or photos. Now what?

No problem. We can suggest a simple visual identity and use professional stock images (Pexels, Unsplash) until you have your own.

Do you work outside Brazil?

Yes. We serve international clients with English communication and USD-based proposals. Reach out to learn more.
